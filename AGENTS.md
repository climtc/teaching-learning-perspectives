# Publication boundary

This is a public visualization repository. The author requires unpublished manuscripts and work in progress to remain accessible locally only until they explicitly authorize publication of the specific upgraded text.

Do not commit or upload private manuscript originals, extracts, draft titles, filenames, absolute paths, hashes, span indexes, prompt histories, or summaries revealing private draft content. Keep source-to-case mappings and draft revision plans outside this repository in local-only storage.

Public artifacts may contain interactive experiments, usage explanations, openly available datasets and literature, and bibliographic references to published books. Inspect the entire staged diff before publication. A completed implementation does not authorize publication of its private manuscript evidence.
